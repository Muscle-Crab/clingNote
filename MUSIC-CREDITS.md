# Background music credits & licenses

All ClingNote background music is **original and procedurally generated at runtime** with the Web Audio API
(`src/composables/useBackgroundAudio.js`). No audio samples, recordings, or third-party compositions are bundled or streamed.
Chord progressions used are common, non-copyrightable harmonic patterns. No attribution required; bundle size impact ~0 (a few KB of JS).
