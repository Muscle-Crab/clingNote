# Manager & Pal — how it works

## Manager links (one-way, no account)
**Creating one.** In the app, open **Manager & Pals → Manager**, enter what you call the person (e.g. "Mom"), and tap **Create link**. Share it with the share sheet or copy it. It looks like `https://<app>/m/<secret>`. The secret is long and random, and the app stores only a scrambled (hashed) copy of it. The full link is shown only on the device that made it, so if you lose it, tap **New link**. That gives you a fresh link and kills the old one.

**Turning it off.** **Turn off** revokes the link right away. Anyone who opens it afterwards sees "Link not available."

**Using it (manager side).** The manager taps the link and lands straight on your task list. There's no sign-up or password. The first time, they're asked "What should we call you?", and that's it.

**What the manager can see:** your tasks for the week (title, day, done or not), with **overdue tasks at the top**, then to-do, then done. That's all. They can't see anything else in your account, and they can't add, edit, or delete tasks.

**What the manager can do:**
- Tap **Ping** or **Remind** on any task. It's one tap and shows "Sent ✓" instantly.
- Tap **Send encouragement**.
- Message you on the **Messages** tab, by typing or tapping a quick message chip.

## Pings & DMs (your side)
Nudges and messages from your manager show up in **Manager & Pals** with a red badge on the menu. You also get a notification where notifications are already set up. You can reply from the same screen (there are quick-reply chips too), and your manager sees the reply the next time their page refreshes (about every 15 seconds). Opening the Manager tab marks everything as read.

## Pals (mutual)
1. One person opens **Manager & Pals → Pals** and taps **Get invite code**. They get an 8-character code that works once and expires after 7 days.
2. They send the code (or link) to a friend, who types it under **Have a code?** and taps **Connect**.
3. You're now pals. Each of you sees the other's tasks **live**, so changes appear instantly, and you can chat in real time.
4. Either of you can tap **Remove pal** at any time. That disconnects you both and deletes your chat.
